I like systems that fail loudly and recover quietly. Most of what I build sits below the application layer — write-ahead logs, memtables, compaction schedulers, consensus.

The through-line is durability under pressure: what happens when the process dies mid-write, when the disk fills, when four SSTables pile up and something has to merge them without pulling a gigabyte into RAM. Those are the questions I find genuinely fun.

Currently a CS student specializing in data engineering. When I'm not writing C++ or Go, I'm probably reading a paper about compaction strategies I don't need yet.

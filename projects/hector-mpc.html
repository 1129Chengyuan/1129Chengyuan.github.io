Bipedal Robot Control on GPU

robotics simulation · C++ → PyTorch · Georgia Tech LIDAR, Dec 2024 – May 2025

Problem

HECTOR is a bipedal robot studied in Georgia Tech’s Laboratory for Intelligent Decision and Autonomous Robots. Its controller (Model Predictive Control, which plans the robot’s next movements by solving a small optimization problem many times a second) was written in C++ for the CPU, which capped the lab at about 120 simulations running at once.

Approach

Working with PhD students in the lab, I converted the core control logic from C++ to PyTorch so it could run on a GPU. The GPU version advances thousands of simulated robots together in batches.

Under the hood: the robot dynamics and the optimization step were rewritten as batched tensor operations in PyTorch, so each step of the controller is computed for every simulation in parallel on the GPU.

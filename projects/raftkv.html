RaftKV

distributed kv · in progress
Tracked and under active build. Everything the tree, graph, and terminal show for this node comes from its entry in assets/site.js — edit there and it updates everywhere.

A replicated key-value store on top of the Raft consensus protocol — leader election, log replication, and a state machine that applies committed entries. The point is to feel, first-hand, what consensus costs and why every distributed system reaches for it anyway.

Tracked and under active build. Everything the tree, graph, and terminal show for this node comes from its entry in assets/site.js — edit there and it updates everywhere.

The front door of the pipeline: a Kafka consumer that pulls raw events off a topic, decodes Avro payloads, and hands clean records downstream to Streamline. The problems worth solving here are backpressure and offset management — committing an offset only after a record is durably passed on, so a crash replays instead of dropping.
